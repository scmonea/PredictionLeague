How to use it

  • Pick a display name the first time you visit -- that's your identity for the season. Typing that same name in again later (even on a different phone or browser) signs you straight back into that same account and picks, a bit like a login -- so if you get logged out, just re-enter the name you used before rather than picking a new one.
  • On Predict, enter the score you think each fixture will end -- it saves automatically as soon as you move to the next field, no button to remember.
  • Change your mind as many times as you like -- a pick only locks the moment that fixture kicks off.
  • Click any name on the Leaderboard to see their full pick history -- your own is always visible; everyone else's picks for a fixture unlock once it's kicked off. Picks stay there for the whole season, so this doubles as your own history too.

Scoring

Every match has odds for each result (home win / draw / away win), set by bookmakers. When you predict a scoreline, you're backing one of those three outcomes. How many points you get depends on how unlikely your pick was and how close you get.

  • Wrong side entirely (e.g. you picked a home win, away won) → 0 points, no matter how close the score.
  • Right side, any score → the decimal odds you backed, rounded down.
  • Right side and the goal difference also matches (even if the score itself isn't exact) → bonus points based on the margin: a 2-goal margin is worth +1, 3 is worth +2, up to a cap of +5 for a 6+ goal margin. A margin of 0-1 (most results) earns nothing here.
  • Right side and the exact score too → +3 more on top of that.

Fixtures & odds

  • Fixtures and odds refresh several times through the week, pulling real bookmaker prices -- so a fixture without odds yet still gets picked up automatically before kickoff, no games get left off.
  • Normally around 10 fixtures show up at once -- all 20 Premier League clubs play once a gameweek.
  • The odds shown when your pick saves are locked to it permanently -- odds moving afterwards, or a later refresh, can never change what you're scored against.
  • Each fixture locks independently at its own kickoff, not the whole gameweek at once.
